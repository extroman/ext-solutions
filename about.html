ABOUT

Who's behind EXT Solutions

EXT Solutions is a one-person operation built on real trade experience, not a background in corporate consulting.

Background

Alberta-based, with a work history spanning industrial trades, construction, drilling support, and transport logistics — including extended stretches at remote sites and camps across northern Canada. That range of hands-on site experience is what shapes the paperwork and guides built here: they come from having actually worked the jobs, not from a template.

Credentials & Training

Training includes Occupational First Aid, CPR/AED, H2S Alive, WHMIS, fall protection, and several other site-safety certifications picked up across different industries. Currently completing a college diploma in Emergency Management, adding a formal planning and preparedness foundation on top of the practical trade background.

Why EXT Solutions

Most safety paperwork and preparedness content is written by people who've never actually done the work it describes. EXT Solutions exists to close that gap — straightforward documentation and honest preparedness guidance, priced and written for real sites and real households.
