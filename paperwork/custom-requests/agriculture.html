Agriculture & Farm

Family and hobby farms — the Farm Safety Check and OHS Farm Safety Guide live here, plus custom paperwork if your setup needs more.

Ready-made for farms

Two things already exist for this sector — the Farm Safety Check ($12) and the OHS Farm Safety Guide ($35). Start there before requesting anything custom.

What to send over

Site type, crew size, and the main hazards you're already tracking. If you've got an existing FLHA or safety doc, send it along — it's faster to build from something than from a blank page.

Typical turnaround

Most requests in this category are scoped within a day and delivered within a week, depending on how many documents are involved.
