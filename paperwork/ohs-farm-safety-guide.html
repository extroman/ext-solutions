OHS Farm Safety Guide

A stripped-down farm safety guide covering the bare minimum — a simple disaster plan, a muster point, and what actually needs to be in place.

What you get

A plain-language farm safety guide built around what's realistic for a working farm — not a full OHS binder nobody has time to implement. Covers a simple disaster plan, a clear muster point, and the handful of things that actually matter.

Why it's simple on purpose

There's a lot more a farm could be doing for safety, but most won't — that's just how the industry runs day to day. This guide sticks to the bare minimum that's realistic to actually put in place, instead of a checklist that gets filed away and ignored.

What's included

A basic compliance review of your setup against the guide is included, so you know exactly where you stand once you've gone through it.
