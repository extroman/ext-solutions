Farm Safety Check

A quick, plain-language check on whether your farm is actually ready for an emergency — not a binder full of paperwork, just what actually matters.

What you get

A one-page checklist that walks through whether your farm is actually set up for an emergency — is there a stocked first aid kit anyone can find, an evacuation plan for people and livestock, basic fire and severe weather prep. Built for family and hobby farms, not a 40-page corporate binder nobody has time to read.

Why it's different

Most farm safety resources are written for large commercial operations with a full-time safety officer. This is for the kind of place where it's just you and maybe a hired hand — a straight list of what's actually missing, not compliance jargon.

How it works

Answer the checklist yourself in about 10 minutes. You're left with a plain list of what to fix first.

Full disclosure

The Canadian Agricultural Safety Association offers a complete FarmSafe Plan for free. This isn't trying to replace that — it's the fast version for someone who wants a 10-minute gut-check instead of working through a full binder.

Need more than the checklist?

If your farm needs a full walkthrough and complete compliance review rather than the $12 self-check, reach out directly and we'll scope it out.
