HRVA Assessments

Hazard, Risk, and Vulnerability Assessments — the foundation Alberta's emergency management programs are built on.

What an HRVA is

A Hazard, Risk, and Vulnerability Assessment (HRVA) is the quantitative and qualitative foundation Alberta communities use to build emergency management programs. It's the framework the Alberta Emergency Management Agency (AEMA) and local municipalities use to systematically identify, analyze, and prioritize disaster risks.

What you get

A structured assessment identifying the hazards relevant to your community or organization, scoring likelihood and consequence, and flagging vulnerabilities — built to align with the same framework municipalities and AEMA use.

Best for

Municipalities, community organizations, and larger operations that need a formal risk foundation for emergency planning — a bigger scope than the Farm Safety Check or a single-site FLHA.

Pricing

Scope varies a lot by community size and hazard complexity, so this one's quoted per request rather than a flat rate.
