Seasonal Checklists

Alberta-specific seasonal prep — what to check before winter hits and what gets forgotten every year.

Fall (before the cold hits)

  • Test the furnace before you need it, change the filter
  • Check weatherstripping on doors and windows
  • Clean gutters before the first freeze
  • Swap the emergency kit's summer items for winter ones
  • Check block heater and vehicle battery before it gets cold

Winter

  • Keep a winter kit in every vehicle — blanket, boots, sand/kitty litter, booster cables
  • Know your plan if the power goes out for an extended period
  • Check smoke and CO detector batteries (more indoor heating = more risk)
  • Have a way to heat part of the house without power, safely

Spring / Summer (brief)

  • Check for overland flooding risk if you're on lower ground
  • Wildfire season prep if you're rural — defensible space around structures, go-bag ready
  • Re-check expiry dates on kit food and medications
