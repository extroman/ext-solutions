Reading List

A short, real list of books and journals worth your time in disaster and emergency management.

Books

  • The Unthinkable — Amanda Ripley. Why some people survive disasters and others don't; widely cited outside the field too.
  • A Paradise Built in Hell — Rebecca Solnit. How communities actually behave after disaster, against the myth of panic and chaos.
  • Introduction to Emergency Management — Haddow, Bullock & Coppola. The standard field textbook, used across EM diploma and degree programs.
  • Five Days at Memorial — Sheri Fink. A hospital during Hurricane Katrina; hard look at real decisions under pressure.
  • Deep Survival — Laurence Gonzales. Why some people survive extreme situations and others don't, at the individual level.

Journals (for anyone going deeper)

  • International Journal of Disaster Risk Reduction
  • Disaster Prevention and Management
  • Journal of Emergency Management
  • Natural Hazards
