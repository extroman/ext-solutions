Home & Vehicle Kits

A real 72-hour kit checklist, based on the Government of Canada's Get Prepared guide — trimmed down to what's actually worth packing.

Water & food (3 days)

  • 4 litres of water per person per day (2 for drinking, 2 for hygiene)
  • Non-perishable food — canned goods, dried food, energy bars
  • Manual can opener
  • Check and rotate food/water every 12 months

Core gear

  • Crank or battery flashlight, extra batteries
  • Battery or crank AM/FM radio
  • First aid kit and essential medications
  • Battery pack / power bank, phone charger
  • Warm blankets or sleeping bags
  • Whistle, duct tape, garbage bags

Documents & personal

  • Copies of ID, insurance, and emergency contacts
  • Cash in small bills
  • Extra house and vehicle keys
  • Prescription meds and a copy of prescriptions

Vehicle-specific add-ons

  • Ice scraper, small shovel, sand or kitty litter for traction
  • Booster cables
  • Extra warm clothing kept in the vehicle year-round
